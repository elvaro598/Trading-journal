How to Save & Access Your Journal

Run on Ubuntu and open on your Android Phone

Automatic Storage Enabled:

Every trade you log, API key you enter, and filter preference is saved automatically in your browser's LocalStorage memory.

1 Save File Locally on Ubuntu Laptop

Save the HTML file anywhere on your Ubuntu computer (e.g. `~/Downloads/index.html` or `~/Desktop/trading_journal.html`). You can double-click it anytime to open it in Chrome or Firefox!

2 Access on Android Phone (Same Wi-Fi)

To view your trading dashboard on your phone while on the same Wi-Fi network:

# Step A: Open Terminal in Ubuntu folder & start Python server
python3 -m http.server 8000 --bind 0.0.0.0
# Step B: Get your Ubuntu Laptop IP address
hostname -I
# Step C: Open Chrome on your Phone and type:
http://192.168.x.x:8000 (Replace with your actual IP)

3 Add to Phone Home Screen (PWA Experience)

When you open the dashboard link in Chrome on Android, tap the three vertical dots (⋮) in top right and choose "Add to Home screen". It will create an app icon for quick access!
